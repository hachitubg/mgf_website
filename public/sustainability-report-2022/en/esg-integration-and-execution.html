ESG integration into business strategy

In order to realize business goals and relentlessly create sustainable values for society, GREENFEED has rejuvenated its management thinking and integrated sustainable development goals into business strategy which are considered based on three aspects:

  1. Creating lasting value through investing in the drivers
    of sustainability

  2. Creating shared values

  3. Environmental management

Stakeholder engagement

SUPPLIERS

The Group always maintains transparency in
the sharing of information as well as the
evaluation and selection process in order to
develop strong partnerships, particularly with
suppliers who play a vital role in assuring the
supply of raw materials, goods, supplies,
and equipment.

GOVERNMENT AGENCIES

The Group takes a proactive
approach to policy
consultation to ensure full
compliance with legal
regulations and obligations
in business operation as well as
responding to local
community development
programs.

EMPLOYEES

The Group always cares and has practical
policies to ensure the cohesion of
employees, create a good and safe
working environment for employees to
have the opportunity to promote their
potential and advance at work.

INVESTORS

In order to provide the most value to
partners, the Group always maintains
flexible exchange and policy
modifications. Forms of exchange,
communication, and involvement have
also been developed in order to record
feedback and better suit the parties'
growing needs.

CUSTOMERS

Consistent with the Group's motto
"Customer First" the Group
promotes the use of technology to
improve the quality of products
and services to ensure the health
and safety of customers; focuses
on quality management of
after-sales service, and constantly
seeks market to improve customer
experience

ESG management and material topics

Material topics are analyzed based on their impact on business operation across value chain, from
raw materials, animal feed, farm operation, food processing, to product distribution.
Material topics are categorized into economic, environmental, and social pillars to fully reflect
stakeholders’ levels of interests; adding topics of indirect economic impact, assessment of
suppliers’ performance on social, environmental animal welfare management. Material topics are
referenced with the United Nations' 17 Sustainable Development Goals (SDG).
