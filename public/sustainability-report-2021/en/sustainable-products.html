GREENFEED’s raw material assessment process

To ensure products are consistent in quality and meet their intended
specifications, all aspects in the process of production and operation
management such as raw materials and feed rations are strictly regulated.
As a result, GREENFEED provides farmers with high quality and affordable products.


GREENFEED also invests in the storage and management systems
of raw materials and finished goods. It allows GREENFEED to trace any or
all information about a product, including its supplier, origin and
quality of ingredients.

BIOSAFETY & QUALITY ASSURANCE

Superior breed selection

The selection of high-quality breeds is a key to optimizing the cost of manufacture and animal healthcare. The farm operation system is built based on European standards, with biosecurity certification from the Department of Animal Health, VietGAP and Pig Trak traceability.

Efficient and sustainable
farm model

In addition to superior genetic breeds, GREENFEED applied the PIC’s modern breeding process to optimize operation cost, and minimize human errors, ensuring a good health status for the herd.

Biosecurity

Ensuring biosafety is critical to farm operation and performance. Therefore, biosafety control is always emphasized through monitoring management solutions, meeting technical standards and prerequisite control principles.

CUSTOMER’S HEALTH & SAFETY

Each stage of the entire 3F Plus chain is strictly assessed to ensure consumer’s health & safety and the following standards: food safety, traceability through all stages of production, from feed, breed, slaughter to finished product for consumers.

By 2021, GREENFEED has launched many food brands, including G Kitchen, MAMACHOICE, and Wyn, relentlessly bringing good values to millions of Vietnamese families’ meals.
