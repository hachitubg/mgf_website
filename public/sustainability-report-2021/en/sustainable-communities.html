MEALS OF SMILES

Kicked-off in 2019, the “Meals of Smiles” program aims to enhance infant and child nutrition across the country. GREENFEED has been striving towards the

target of 2 million meals by the end of 2022, 3 million meals in 2024, and above all, to ensure every child has the best possible start in life.

EMPOWERING FARMERS

Launched in 2010, the program “Empowering Farmers” of GREENFEED, in partnership with Tuoi Tre Newspaper, has enabled thousands of rural women to afford a better livelihood. The program

not only helps women relieve their financial stress, but also provides them knowledge and skills for livestock healthcare through training sessions with GREENFEED experts.

SHARING GOODNESS TO
FIGHT AGAINST THE PANDEMIC

During COVID-19 pandemic, GREENFEED as a leading Food & Agriculture company proactively implemented the “Share Goodness To Fight Against The Pandemic” program, joining hands with the government, consumers and society to overcome

the challenges. In addition to the VND 20 billion contribution to Vietnam’s COVID-19 Vaccine Fund, the program also provided nutritious food at a stable price, supported food and necessary medical equipment to frontline COVID-19 workers.
