    GREENFEED VIETNAM CONTINUES ITS WINNING RUN WITH TOP 5 PRESTIGIOUS ANIMAL FEED COMPANIES

    Date 2025-07-15

    On January 18, 2024, in Ho Chi Minh City, GREENFEED was honored to receive the double Top 69 in the ranking of Top 500 largest enterprises in Vietnam; Top 5 Animal Feed Companies in the framework of the Ceremony to announce the Top 500 Largest Enterprises in Vietnam (VNR500) and Top 10 Prestigious Companies in Industries in 2023 organized annually by Vietnam Report, VietNamNet Newspaper. This is also the second consecutive year that GREENFEED has received such title.


    ​​​​​​​
    (GREENFEED continues its winning run with Top 5 prestigious animal feed companies)
    Ranked 100th in VNR500 2022, this year, GREENFEED jumped 31 places to reach 69th ranking. This acceleration in the VNR500 rankings and continuously winning prestigious awards throughout 2023 affirms the Group's tireless efforts to become a leading, efficient and trustworthy integrated chain brand, contributing to the prosperous development of the Agriculture - Food industry in particular, and the Vietnamese economy in general.
    ​​​​​​​
    (GREENFEED was honored at the 69th ranking in the list of Top 500 largest enterprises in Vietnam 2023)

    Mr. Ly Anh Duy Quang, Member of the Board of Directors of the GREENFEED attended and received the award and said that “This is an important highlight, marking the growth of the Group after 20 years of development (2003-2023). In 2024, we are still determined to elevate GREENFEED to become a leading, effective, and trustworthy brand across the domestic and global food chain, providing superior technology applications and comprehensive solutions for the partners and customers. GREENFEED's upcoming efforts carry the dreams and missions of a team of 4,500 employees, helping more than 3,000 customers and partners to effectively transform their production and business models, contributing to shaping the market, stabilizing the prices, bringing Healthy and Delicious products to millions of households and putting Vietnam on the map of leading companies in Agriculture - Food in the world".

    ​​​​​​​
    (GREENFEED is determined to elevate GREENFEED to become a leading, effective and trustworthy brand in the entire domestic and global food chain)

    In 2023, due to the increasing demand for clean and delicious food products of animal origin such as meat, milk and eggs, ...etc. the Animal Feed (AF) industry was seen a strong recovery and development. Although the price of animal feed ingredients in the world is on a downward trend, challenges from erratic developments and disease risks still exist.
    ​​​​​​​
    (GREENFEED promotes sustainable procurement practices by perfecting the supply chain from production to consumption)
    According to a survey of animal feed enterprises, Vietnam Report, below is some priority solutions in the near future, specifically:
    •    Focus on building strategic product groups with large sales and high profits
    •    Strengthen measures to manage financial risks, price fluctuations and quality of input materials
    •    Develop a 4F (Farm- Food- Feed- Fertilizer) biosafety model complex
    •    Improve the organizational structure, policies and management of enterprises
    •    Implement sustainable development strategies, environmental protection...

    ​​​​​​​
    (Applying solar power at GREENFEED factory systems to manage and use energy, effectively contributing to the environmental protection)
    Announcement ceremony of the Top 500 Largest Enterprises in Vietnam in 2023 took place successfully with the participation of representatives of the Party and State, more than 500 representatives of leading Vietnamese enterprises, economic experts and representatives of prestigious press and media agencies. Responding to the trend of the times, within the framework of the event, a discussion titled "Leading the digital transformation era: From global lessons to application in Vietnam" was held with the participation of many domestic and foreign experts.
