    OVERVIEW

    In recent years, the livestock farming industry has been strongly affected by market developments and pandemic; in addition, a production management also affects the efficiency of investors. So, there is the shift in livestock farming models, scale of operation as well as competition in production costs.

    Currently, in order to get the efficiency in livestock farming and meet the competition, the livestock farmers must optimize input costs, operational management costs - biosafety and livestock output. With more than 20 years of experience accompanying the livestock industry, GREENFEED has continuously created and developed a 3-FOCUS PILLAR LIVESTOCK FARMING SOLUTION to bring high efficiency to the livestock investors.

    • INPUT

      PROVIDING QUALITY PRODUCTS

    • MANAGEMENT - OPERATION

      HELPING THE CUSTOMERS OPTIMIZE EFFICIENCY - COST

    • OUTPUT

      STABILIZING AND INCREASING THE VALUE

    BREEDS

    In order to optimize livestock productivity and transfer genetic advances to the livestock farmers, GREENFEED has invested in research, selection and cooperation with leading breeding and genetic production partners in the world. Along with the breeding farm systems invested in construction and operation according to standard processes to provide high-quality breeding sources: GF24 gilts; GF337, GF399 commercial pig semen; Isa Warren GF424 high-yield egg-laying hens; GF Ross 308 commercial white chickens; GF201, GF202 high-quality commercial colored chickens. The breeders provided to our customers are GREENFEED's solution to directly transfer genetic advances to livestock farmers to optimize productivity and farming efficiency.

    FEED NUTRITION

    With a capacity of 2.5 million tons/year. GREENFEED's system of 9 animal feed factories meets all the nutritional and product needs of domestic and foreign livestock customers such as: Pigs, poultry, aquatic products and large livestock (cows, goats, etc.)
    Determining nutrition as a particularly important factor in livestock farming, GREENFEED always promotes research programs to apply nutritional technology on livestock such as pigs, poultry and aquatic products, etc.

    Read more

    VETERINARY PRODUCTS

    With the orientation of becoming a leading provider of livestock health care and protection solutions towards sustainable development in Vietnam, GREENFEED has put into operation the Donavet Veterinary Medicine Factory system with a modern production line system and product control process according to GMP standards. In addition to quality and variety of veterinary products, GREENFEED is committed to providing the customers with comprehensive veterinary and biosecurity solutions to optimize the control of herd health.

    TECHNICAL TRANSFER - PERSONNEL TRAINING - OPERATION SUPPORT

    The operation on any livestock farm are very important and also a decisive factor of livestock productivity and efficiency. Through GREENFEED's team of technicians directly participating in advice on operation, transferring the care and feeding management processes, biosafety processes and updating new knowledge to the personnel participating in operating the customers' livestock farm...

    Read more

    DATA MANAGEMENT AND OPERATION TECHNOLOGY SYSTEM

    With the advantage of company directly engaging in information technology and management software, GREENFEED has continuously applied new technologies to support the customers in productivity management and herd health management software: Porcitec software, barn, shelter microclimate management software, farm operation management software (Autofarm), Porcitec, Digifarm.

    HEALTH SCREENING AND DIAGNOSTIC TESTING

    In order to proactively support the diagnosis and ensure food safety and hygiene as well as research and develop the products used in veterinary medicine and food technology. GREENFEED continues to invest in building and putting into operation the GREENFEED BIOLAB Biological Research Center in Long An. Starting with activities of the research center, it will provide the customers with optimal animal health screening and biosafety diagnosis solutions through activities connecting with the GREEN BIOLAB diagnostic and testing center.

    BIOSAFETY MANAGEMENT AND CIRCULAR ECONOMY MODEL TRANSFER TECHNOLOGY

    Biosafety management processes at farms are regularly updated according to actual epidemiological developments and livestock situations. Training and transfer activities are implemented to the farms by the technical teams, and practical solutions are applied in production of livestock farms.

    OPERATIONS MANAGEMENT

    With aiming to ensure to the customers to obtain the operation of livestock equipment systems which is continuously maintained and an overall assessment of safe operation, GREENFEED continuously supports our customers through activities of evaluating, checking, and reviewing machinery systems and livestock equipment by team of highly specialized maintenance staffs from GREENFEED factories. The GREENFEED maintenance team is always ready to connect and implement in line with the plans agreed with the customers.
